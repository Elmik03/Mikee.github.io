Elmik03 projects GitHub: Elmik03

Small tools that work in your browser.

Read a PDF, pull text from a photo, translate Morse, check the weather. Most of these run on your device, so your files stay with you.

Documents

Open a file, get the text or the data out, or read it like a book.

  • PDF to data

    Pull text out of a PDF and save it as CSV, TXT, JSON, SQLite or an HTML table. Scanned pages are read with OCR.

    Runs in your browser
  • PDF to text

    Turn a PDF into plain text. Copy it, or download it as TXT or CSV.

    Runs in your browser
  • Image to text

    Read the text in a photo, screenshot or scan. Works in 13 languages.

    Your image is never uploaded
  • PDF flipbook

    Read a PDF as a book with turning pages, in full screen if you like.

    Your file is never uploaded
  • Books reader

    Open a PDF to read, search, bookmark pages and zoom.

Words and play

Learn a language, find a song, test yourself.

  • Babel

    Learn Spanish, French, German and Italian.

  • Trivia Run

    Pick a category and difficulty, then answer up to twenty questions, twenty seconds each. Fast answers and long streaks score more.

  • Lyric finder

    Search for a song by name, or play a few seconds and let the app work out what it is. Read the lyrics or save them to a file.

  • Morse

    Translate text to Morse and back. Play it as sound, light or vibration, or tap it out on a key.

Everyday

Quick answers and a shared canvas.

  • Weather

    Search for a city and see its weather in °C or °F. Data from Open-Meteo.

  • Roomboard

    A shared drawing board. Send the room code to friends so they can join and draw too.

    Anyone with the code can edit
  • Ask anything

    Chat with a free AI service.

    Answers can be wrong. Don't share private information.

Teams and books

Made for the Upgrade community.

  • Upline

    Turn a team report into a clear tree. Search any ID to see that person and everyone below them.

    PDFs are read in your browser
  • Ask Dream Achievers

    Ask a question about the book by Anthony and Eric Masi. Answers come from the book's text.
